# Artwork and depth maps

- `images/depth/*.png`: 16 custom raster maps made for this Jenga-Code edition using mathematical surfaces and original geometric drawings. Meme subjects are original simplified interpretations of familiar old-web motifs, not downloaded meme files. No third-party image files were copied. Includes 69420, smiley, troll-inspired grin, rage face, sunglasses face, keyboard cat and pixel alien.
- `tools/make-depth-maps.py`: reproducible Pillow source. Lettering was rasterised using DejaVu Sans Bold; font software is not redistributed in this package. Change the font path for your local machine if rebuilding.
- Sphere and torus are surface-depth functions; the remaining images are stylised flat or layered reliefs, not recovered physical 3D scenes. Uploaded photographs are interpreted by brightness, not AI depth estimation.
- `images/optical-study.webp`: original AI-generated editorial artwork created for this project with the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and compressed. Decorative, not part of the depth calculations.
- Prompt: “Editorial engraved illustration for Jenga-Code optical illusion laboratory. Antique wooden stereoscope and two human eyes studied as copperplate scientific engraving, fine navy and charcoal etched linework, small copper accents, warm ivory paper background #f6f0e3, restrained rich detail, portrait composition with generous clean margins. No text, no letters, no labels, no border. Website sidebar artwork, independently designed vintage scientific journal.”
- Compass wordmark: reused from the supplied Jenga-Code design system.

No externally hosted artwork, fonts, analytics or image-generation service is required at runtime.
